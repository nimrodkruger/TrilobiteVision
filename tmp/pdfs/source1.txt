Appendix: Derivation of the probe inverse functions
A. The step probe
The step probe is 𝑑(𝑡)= 𝐴𝑢(𝑡− 𝑡0) (19), giving the per-pixel log-irradiance range Δ𝐿𝑖 = log(1+
𝑤𝑖𝐴/𝐼bg) (20). Time is measured from onset, 𝑡0 = 0, throughout this section, matching the main
text’s convention for (23)-(26). Under the single-pole model (4), the step response is
𝑉 (𝑡)= Δ𝐿𝑖(1− 𝑒{−𝑡/𝜏pr}), 𝑡≥ 0. (S1)
This is the standard first-order step response of 𝜏pr ̇𝑉 + 𝑉 = Δ𝐿𝑖 with 𝑉 (0)= 0.
A.1 Event count 𝑁𝑖
Write 𝑥𝑘 = 𝑒{−𝑡𝑘 /𝜏pr} and 𝑟 = 𝑒{−𝜌/𝜏pr}. No refractory window precedes the first event, so it fires the
instant 𝑉  reaches 𝐶:
𝑉 (𝑡1)= 𝐶⟹ Δ𝐿𝑖(1− 𝑥1)= 𝐶⟹ 𝑥1 = 1− 𝐶/Δ𝐿𝑖, (S2)
matching the initial condition quoted for (23). For 𝑘 ≥ 1, the reference resets to 𝑉 (𝑡𝑘 + 𝜌) (6), and
the (𝑘 + 1)-th event fires when 𝑉  has climbed a further 𝐶 above it:
𝑉 (𝑡{𝑘+1})− 𝑉 (𝑡𝑘 + 𝜌)= 𝐶. (S3)
Substitute the step response into both terms. Since 𝑒{−(𝑡𝑘 +𝜌)/𝜏pr} = 𝑟𝑥𝑘,
Δ𝐿𝑖(1− 𝑥{𝑘+1})− Δ𝐿𝑖(1−𝑟𝑥𝑘 )= 𝐶⟹ 𝑥{𝑘+1} = 𝑟𝑥𝑘 − 𝐶/Δ𝐿𝑖, (S4)
which is (23), with 𝑥1 = 1− 𝐶/Δ𝐿𝑖 as above.
This is a linear (affine) recursion, not a bare geometric one: writing 𝛽 ≡ 𝐶/Δ𝐿𝑖, it is 𝑥{𝑘+1} =
𝑟𝑥𝑘 − 𝛽. Its fixed point is
𝑥∗ = −𝛽/(1− 𝑟), (S5)
found by setting 𝑥∗ = 𝑟𝑥∗ − 𝛽. The deviation 𝜑𝑘 ≡ 𝑥𝑘 − 𝑥∗ obeys the homogeneous recursion
𝜑{𝑘+1} = 𝑟𝜑𝑘, so
𝑥𝑘 = 𝑥∗ + (𝑥1 − 𝑥∗)𝑟{𝑘−1}. (S6)
The burst ends at the count 𝑁𝑖 where 𝑥{𝑁𝑖} reaches zero — the source-follower has settled and no
further crossing occurs. Setting 𝑥𝑁 = 0 and solving for 𝑁 (treated as continuous, “taking the limit”
of the recursion) gives, after substituting 𝑥1 = 1− 𝛽,
𝑟{𝑁−1} = 𝛽/[(1− 𝑟)+ 𝛽𝑟]⟹ 𝑁 = 1+ 𝜏pr/𝜌⋅ log(𝑟 + Δ𝐿𝑖(1− 𝑟)/𝐶). (S7)
This is exact for the recursion above. In the regime relevant to real sensors — many refractory
periods elapse before the burst ends, 𝜌≪ 𝜏pr, so 𝑁𝑖 ≫ 1 and 𝑟 = 𝑒{−𝜌/𝜏pr} ≈ 1 — the leading “+1” is
negligible against 𝑁𝑖 itself, and 𝑟 may be replaced by 1 inside the logarithm (the exact refractory
factor (1− 𝑟) is kept, not linearised). Equation 7 then reduces to (24),
𝑁𝑖 = 𝜏pr/𝜌⋅ log(1+ Δ𝐿𝑖(1−𝑒{−𝜌/𝜏pr})/𝐶). (S8)
Both forms agree closely once 𝜌/𝜏pr ≪ 1 (checked numerically: at 𝜌/𝜏pr = 0.01 they differ by under
1% in 𝑁𝑖; the “+1”/exact-recursion form should be preferred if 𝜌 is not small compared to 𝜏pr).
Inverting (24) for the optical weight gives (25),

B. The linear ramp
The ramp is 𝑑(𝑡)= 𝛼(𝑡− 𝑡0) (27). Writing 𝑠= 𝑡− 𝑡0 and collecting the per-pixel terms into a

the irradiance is 𝐼𝑖(𝑠)= 𝐼bg + 𝑤𝑖𝛼𝑠= 𝐼bg(1+𝑠/𝑇𝑖), so, as in the main text’s (29),
𝐿𝑖(𝑠)= log 𝐼bg + log(1+ 𝑠/𝑇𝑖), (S15)
a function of 𝑠/𝑇𝑖 alone: the weight sets a time scale, not an amplitude, unlike the step. Every
statistic below estimates 𝑇𝑖, then recovers 𝑤𝑖 = 𝐼bg/(𝛼𝑇𝑖).
